========================================================================================
HR JOB REQUIREMENT CATEGORY MAPPING REFERENCE
========================================================================================

This reference file defines how HR Job Descriptions (Standard Requirements) and 
HR Hidden Requirements are categorized into the 5 core evaluation dimensions for candidate screening.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. seniority_title (Position & Seniority Match — Weight: 15%)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Target Job Title & Position applied (e.g., "Nhân viên C&B", "Senior Backend Engineer", "Trưởng phòng HR").
- Career Level (e.g., Junior, Mid-level, Senior, Lead, Manager, Director).
- Total Years of Overall Work Experience required (e.g., "Tối thiểu 3 năm kinh nghiệm làm việc").
- Field/Domain-Specific Years of Experience required (e.g., "Ưu tiên có kinh nghiệm C&B từ 1-2 năm trở lên", "Có 2+ năm làm việc trong ngành Fintech").
- Management, Team Leadership, or Operational scope expectations.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. technical_skills (Technical Skills & Competencies — Weight: 30%)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core Technical Skills, Hard Skills, and domain expertise.
- Programming Languages, Frameworks, Libraries (e.g., Python, Java, React, FastAPI).
- Databases, Cloud Platforms, Infrastructure Tools (e.g., PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS).
- Specialized Software, HRMS systems, ERPs, Excel proficiency, Technical methodologies.
- Core technical competencies required for daily job execution.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. work_experience (Work Experience & Project Relevance — Weight: 30%)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Specific Role Responsibilities, Work Scope, and daily duties.
- Industry exposure and domain background (e.g., retail, e-commerce, banking).
- Project scale, traffic handling, team size, budget management.
- Key Achievements, quantifiable outcomes, problem-solving history.
- Practical working background framing daily tasks and responsibilities.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. education_certifications (Education & Certifications — Weight: 10%)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Academic Degree requirements (e.g., Bachelor, Master, Engineering Degree).
- Major / Field of Study (e.g., Computer Science, HR Management, Business Administration).
- GPA or Academic honors/classification (e.g., GPA >= 3.2, Bằng Khá/Giỏi).
- Professional Certifications (e.g., PMP, AWS Certified, SHRM, ACCA).
- Foreign Language Certificates & Scores (e.g., TOEIC >= 650, IELTS >= 6.5, JLPT N2, HSK 5).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. hidden_culture (HR Hidden Requirements & Culture Fit — Weight: 15%)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Workplace Attitude, Mindset, and Personal Values.
- Soft Skills (e.g., Communication, Teamwork, Problem-solving, Time Management).
- Stress Tolerance, Adaptability, Work Pressure management, Agility.
- Work Arrangement / Employment conditions (e.g., Willingness to OT, On-site vs Hybrid, Travel requirement).
- Corporate Culture alignment, Proactiveness, Long-term commitment expectation.
========================================================================================
