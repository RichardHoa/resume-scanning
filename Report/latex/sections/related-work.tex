\section{Related Work}
\noindent\textit{Author: Mã Thẩm Hằng}


This section surveys prior work on automated CV parsing, resume--job matching,
and the use of large language models in hiring. We also review studies on
fairness, robustness, and explainability in AI-driven recruitment, and
position our system relative to commercial Applicant Tracking Systems and
multilingual NLP approaches for low-resource languages such as Vietnamese.
