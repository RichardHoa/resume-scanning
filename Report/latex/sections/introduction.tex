\section{Introduction}
\noindent\textit{Author: Mã Thẩm Hằng}


Recruiters in Vietnam and elsewhere face a growing volume of CVs, and manual
screening is slow, inconsistent, and prone to unconscious bias. Existing
Applicant Tracking Systems handle Vietnamese CVs poorly, rely on keyword
matching rather than semantic understanding, offer little explanation for
their scores, and often depend on third-party APIs that raise data-privacy
concerns. This report presents an end-to-end recruitment support system built
on open-source large language models that parses Vietnamese and English CVs,
assesses job fit, and produces interpretable scores.
